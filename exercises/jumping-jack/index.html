How to do a jumping jack

Land softly on the balls of your feet.

With coach Ana

Works
Heart rate, legs
Beginners start with
a 20 to 30 second hold
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Stand tall Feet together, arms by your sides.
  2. Jump out Feet wide as your arms sweep overhead.
  3. Jump back in Land softly on the balls of your feet.
