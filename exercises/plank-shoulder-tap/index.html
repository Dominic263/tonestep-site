How to do a plank shoulder tap

Feet wide. Hips stay still while you tap.

With coach Lena

Works
Shoulders, core, triceps
Beginners start with
8 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. High plank Hands under shoulders, feet wider than hips.
  2. Tap one shoulder With the opposite hand, hips level.
  3. Switch hands Slow and steady.
