How to do an inchworm

Bend your knees as much as you need.

With coach Ana

Works
Hamstrings, shoulders
Beginners start with
5 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Stand tall Feet hip-width apart.
  2. Hinge and reach down Bend your knees as much as you need.
  3. Walk your hands out Until you're in a plank.
  4. Walk them back Then roll up slowly to standing.
