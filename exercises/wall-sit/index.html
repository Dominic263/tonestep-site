How to do a wall sit

Slide down only as far as feels good.

With coach Jess

Works
Thighs
Beginners start with
a 20 to 30 second hold
Equipment
A wall or sturdy surface

Step by step

  1. Back against a wall Feet out in front, hip-width apart.
  2. Slide down Until your knees are bent to about ninety degrees.
  3. Knees over ankles Back flat on the wall. Hold and breathe.
