How to do a mountain climber

Hands under shoulders. Knees in, nice and steady.

With coach Ana

Works
Core, heart rate
Beginners start with
a 20 to 30 second hold
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. High plank Hands under shoulders, body in a straight line.
  2. Knees in, nice and steady One knee toward your chest, then the other.
  3. Hips stay low Level with your shoulders, like a plank.
