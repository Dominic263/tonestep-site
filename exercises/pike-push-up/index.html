How to do a pike push-up

Hips high. Lower the top of your head toward the floor.

With coach Sarah

Works
Shoulders, triceps
Beginners start with
6 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Hips high Hands and feet on the mat, body in an upside-down V.
  2. Look at your feet Head between your arms, weight over your hands.
  3. Lower the top of your head Bend your elbows toward the mat in front of your hands.
  4. Press back up Drive through your shoulders, hips stay high.
