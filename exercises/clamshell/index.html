How to do a clamshell

Feet together. Open your top knee like a book.

With coach Sarah

Works
Outer glutes, hips
Beginners start with
12 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Lie on your side Knees bent, feet together, head on your arm.
  2. Open your top knee Feet stay touching, hips stay stacked.
  3. Close slowly Then switch sides.
