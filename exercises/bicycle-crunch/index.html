How to do a bicycle crunch

Elbow to the opposite knee. Keep it slow.

With coach Lena

Works
Obliques, abs
Beginners start with
10 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back Hands behind your head, shoulders lifted.
  2. Twist to one side Elbow toward the opposite knee, other leg long.
  3. Switch sides Smoothly, like pedalling.
