How to do a curtsy lunge

Step back and across. Hips stay square.

With coach Maya

Works
Glutes, outer hips, quads
Beginners start with
8 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Stand tall Feet hip-width, hands on your hips.
  2. Step back and across One foot behind the other, like a curtsy.
  3. Bend both knees Lower until your front thigh is nearly level.
  4. Push back up Through your front heel, then switch sides.
