How to do a hollow hold

Lower back down. Tuck your knees to make it easier.

With coach Lena

Works
Abs
Beginners start with
a 20 to 30 second hold
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back Press your lower back into the floor.
  2. Lift your shoulders and legs Arms reach past your hips or overhead.
  3. Lower back stays down Bend your knees if it starts to lift.
  4. Hold and breathe Small, steady breaths.

Make it easier or harder

EasierSide plank
