How to do a single-leg glute bridge

Keep your hips level as you lift.

With coach Sarah

Works
Glutes, hamstrings
Beginners start with
8 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back One foot flat, the other leg straight up.
  2. Push through your heel Lift your hips until they're level.
  3. Lower slowly Then switch legs.
