How to do a sumo squat

Knees push out over your toes.

With coach Sarah

Works
Glutes, inner thighs, quads
Beginners start with
12 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Stand wide Feet wider than your hips, toes turned out.
  2. Sit down between your heels Knees track over your toes, chest tall.
  3. Drive back up Squeeze your glutes at the top.
