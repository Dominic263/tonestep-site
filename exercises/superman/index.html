How to do a superman

Lift a little, hold a beat, lower slowly.

With coach Sarah

Works
Lower back, glutes
Beginners start with
10 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Lie face down Arms stretched long past your head, legs straight.
  2. Lift a little Arms, chest and legs, just off the mat.
  3. Hold a beat Neck long, eyes on the mat.
  4. Lower slowly Everything together.
