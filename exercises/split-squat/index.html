How to do a split squat

Stay tall and drop straight down.

With coach Jess

Works
Thighs, glutes, balance
Beginners start with
8 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Take a long stride One foot forward, one back, back heel lifted.
  2. Stay tall Hips square, hands on your hips.
  3. Drop straight down Back knee toward the floor, front knee over the ankle.
  4. Drive back up Through your front heel. Your feet stay put.

Make it easier or harder

EasierReverse lunge
