How to do a reverse crunch

Curl your hips off the mat. Slow on the way down.

With coach Lena

Works
Lower abs
Beginners start with
10 reps
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back Knees bent over your hips, arms by your sides.
  2. Curl your hips up Knees toward your chest, lower back off the mat.
  3. Lower slowly Back to the start, no swinging.
