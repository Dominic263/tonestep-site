How to do a side plank

Lift your hips. Knee down is a great place to start.

With coach Sarah

Works
Obliques, hips
Beginners start with
a 20 to 30 second hold
Equipment
None

Step by step

  1. Lie on your side Elbow under your shoulder, legs stacked.
  2. Lift your hips Make a straight line from head to feet.
  3. Knee down is a great place to start Bottom knee on the mat, same straight line.
  4. Hold and breathe Then switch sides.
