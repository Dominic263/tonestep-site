Frequently asked questions

Is calisthenics good for women?

Yes. Calisthenics is strength training with your own bodyweight: push-ups, squats, lunges, planks and bridges. The CDC recommends muscle-strengthening activity for all major muscle groups at least two days a week, and bodyweight training counts. It needs no gym and scales to any starting point.

Can a complete beginner do calisthenics?

Yes, if every move starts easy enough. Tonestep puts each exercise on a ladder: push-ups begin against a wall, then a counter, then your knees, then the floor. You move up a rung when a move feels easy.

How long are the workouts?

About 20 minutes on your training days, with 10-minute versions for busy days. Your first week uses fewer rounds while you find your feet.

Do I need any equipment?

No. A little floor space is enough; a wall and a sturdy chair or couch help for the easier rungs.

Is there a quiet option for apartments?

Yes. Quiet mode swaps jumping moves for low-impact ones, so downstairs neighbours and sleeping kids stay happy.
