Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 (updated for RevenueCat and Superwall)

Tonestep is a home workout app. It is built to work without knowing who you are: there is no account, no sign-in and no server that stores your training. Your workouts, photos and measurements never leave your phone. The only data that leaves it is what two services need to sell and show the membership, described under Purchases below.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into Tonestep is stored only on your iPhone:

  • your setup answers (first name, goals, training days, things to go easy on)
  • your workouts, reps, streaks and progress
  • body check-in photos and tape measurements
  • reminder times and settings

Body check-in photos are kept inside the app, protected while your phone is locked, left out of device backups and never added to your photo library. Deleting a check-in deletes its photos. Deleting the app deletes everything.

The camera

The camera is used for two things, only when you start them: counting your reps during a set, and taking body check-in photos. Rep counting reads each frame on your phone and discards it; no video is recorded, kept or sent. Check-in photos are saved on your phone as described above.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Tonestep saves each finished workout (its type, time and estimated energy) to Health. It does not read anything from Health. Health data is never used for advertising and never sold.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification service receives your data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details.

To know whether your membership is active, Tonestep uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history from Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

The membership screen can be shown by Superwall, which lets us improve that screen without an app update. Superwall receives an anonymous device identifier, your device model, language and approximate region (worked out from your IP address), whether you're a member, and how you use the membership screen. See Superwall's privacy policy.

Neither receives your workouts, photos, measurements or Health data, and neither links this data to your identity.

What we don't do

No tracking across apps or websites. No advertising. No selling or sharing of personal data. No analytics beyond how the membership screen is used.

Children

Tonestep is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
