Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email hello@tonestep.app. We read everything and usually reply within a day or two.

Cancel or change your membership

Memberships are billed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tonestep. In the app: Profile → Membership → Manage or cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of a period (or of the free trial) and you won't be charged again.

Restore a purchase

New phone, or reinstalled? Open the membership screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone; there's no account to delete. Deleting the app deletes your workouts, photos and measurements. Body check-ins can be deleted one by one in Progress.

The camera won't count my reps

Stand your phone up so your whole body is in the frame, in good light. For push-ups, put it about 2 m (6 ft) away.
